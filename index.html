The Coffee Avenue Experience

Meet Your
Rocking Barista.

This isn't just a café — it's a vibe. Perched on the 2nd floor of the Old Concord building on Satmasjid Road, Coffee Avenue is where Dhanmondi comes to breathe. Students pull all-nighters here. Couples find their corner. Professionals close deals over a Butterscotch Mocha.

At the heart of it all? Our barista. Part artist, part therapist, full-time coffee wizard. They'll remember your order, debate your taste in music, and pour a latte art that's actually too pretty to drink (but you will anyway).
